## Theme 50 · p.124 | 본문 | 기펜재의 정의 → 가정이 있음 → ① 열등재 → ② 소득의 큰 몫 → ③ 대체재 없음 → 빵엔 더 싼 주식이 없음 → 빵값 상승의 두 효과 → 덜 삼 → 대체 효과 = 일반 법칙 → 그러나 소득 효과로 수요 증가 → 소득 효과가 이김 → 다른 예: 감자 대기근
Today, commodities for which demand rises as their prices rise are known as Giffen goods. | 첫 문장: 정의 — 값이 오르면 수요도 오르는 기펜재
Giffen goods rely on a number of assumptions. | 기펜재가 성립하는 가정들(예고)
First, the commodity has to be an inferior good, that is, a good that people choose to buy less of as their income rises because there are better alternatives; for example, choosing meat over bread. | 'First' = 조건 ① 열등재
Second, the consumer must spend a large portion of their income on this product, hence the fact that the example refers to the poorest section of society. | 'Second' = 조건 ② 소득의 큰 몫
Finally, there must be no alternatives to the product. | 'Finally' = 조건 ③ 대체재가 없음
In the case of bread, there is no cheaper alternative staple. | 조건 ③을 빵에: 더 싼 주식이 없음
Given these assumptions, an increase in the price of bread creates two effects. | 새 단락: 'these assumptions' = 세 조건 → 두 효과 예고
It causes people to buy less bread because the satisfaction it creates per pound of spending falls compared to other goods. | 효과 ①: 빵을 덜 삼(만족도 하락)
This substitution effect would cause bread to follow the general rule of higher price causing lower demand. | 'This substitution effect' = 효과 ①의 이름 → 일반 법칙대로
However, as the price of bread rises, it also reduces the power to spend on other things, and because bread is an inferior good, this lower income will make the demand for bread rise. | 'However ... also' = 효과 ②: 쓸 돈이 줄어 빵 수요 증가

## Theme 51 · p.128 | 본문 | 재산권이 자원 이용을 좌우 → 넉넉하면 상관없음 → 희소해지면 변화 시작 → 수요·공급으로 보자 → 재산권 수요가 생기는 때 → 가상 마을(수요 낮음) → 외부 변화로 수요 급증 → 공동 이용 계속 → 서로 손실 → 제도 변화 요구 → 일반화: 시장 실패
Property rights are key determinants of natural resource use. | 첫 문장: 재산권이 자원 이용을 좌우
When a natural resource is not scarce relative to the existing demand, the rights to use it are generally irrelevant. | 자원이 넉넉하면 → 사용권은 상관없음
If changes occur that render the resource economically scarce, however, e.g., shifts in preferences, technology, or demographics, rights to use the resource begin to matter and a process whereby property rights evolve may be set in motion. | 'however' = 희소해지면 → 권리가 중요, 변화 시작
It is useful to think of this process in terms of supply and demand forces. | 'this process' = 재산권 변화 → 수요·공급으로 보자
The demand for property rights arises when groups or individuals realize that their welfare would be enhanced if the current property rights system were changed. | 새 단락: 재산권 '수요'가 생기는 때
Imagine a renewable natural resource used by a village under open access conditions in which demand is low relative to the yield the resource can sustain, so common pool losses are minimal. | 'Imagine' = 가상 사례: 수요가 낮은 마을 자원

Users now have an incentive to “demand” a change in the property rights system, to a form that avoids these losses. | 'these losses' = 손실을 피하려 제도 변화 '요구'
